# Customer SLA

The contractual service level agreement covers the tracking API only.

- **Commitment:** 99.5% monthly availability.
- **Credits:** 10% of the monthly fee if availability falls below 99.5%, and
  25% if it falls below 99.0%.
- **Exclusions:** scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours ahead.

The SLA is a contract with customers; the per-service SLOs are internal
targets, set tighter than the SLA.
