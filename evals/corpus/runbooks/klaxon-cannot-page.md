# Runbook: Klaxon cannot send pages

Use this when Klaxon is down or its pages are not arriving.

1. Confirm the failure on Klaxon's status dashboard.
2. If pages have been failing for 10 minutes, the incident commander starts the
   phone tree: each team's engineering manager phones their primary on-call.
3. Post in #incidents that paging is down, so engineers watch the channel.
4. When Klaxon recovers, check that every page sent during the outage was
   acknowledged.
