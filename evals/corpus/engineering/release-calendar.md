# Release calendar

## Weekly release train

The release train is cut every Wednesday at 12:00 Pacific time. Anything merged
before the cut ships in that week's deploys.

## Holiday code freeze

The 2025 holiday code freeze runs from 15 December 2025 to 5 January 2026.
During the freeze only fixes for SEV1 and SEV2 incidents may be deployed, and
each needs a director's approval.
