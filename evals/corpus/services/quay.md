# Quay (carrier onboarding portal)

Quay is where new carrier partners register, receive their credentials, and
certify their integration before sending production events to Dockhand.

## Ownership

- **Team:** Partnerships
- **Language:** Python, with Django
- **On-call rotation:** none; issues are handled in business hours.

## Certification

A carrier is certified after it sends 3 valid test scans to Dockhand's sandbox
endpoint. Certification is reviewed by the Partnerships team within 2 business
days. Quay's SLO is 99.0% monthly availability.
