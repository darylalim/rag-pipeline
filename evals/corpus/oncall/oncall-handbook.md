# On-call handbook

## Rotations

Each team runs a weekly rotation with a primary and a secondary on-call.
Rotations hand off on Tuesdays at 10:00 Pacific time, so a handoff never falls
on a weekend or a Monday holiday.

## Escalation

Pages go to the primary first. If the primary has not acknowledged within 5
minutes, the page escalates to the secondary; after 15 minutes without an
acknowledgement it escalates to the team's engineering manager.

## Tools

Pages are sent by Klaxon, Tallowmere's internal paging tool. Every page links to
the alert's runbook.

## Handoff

The outgoing primary writes a handoff note listing open incidents, silenced
alerts and anything expected in the coming week.
