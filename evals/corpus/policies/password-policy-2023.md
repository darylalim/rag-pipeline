# Password policy (2023)

**Status:** superseded by the authentication policy in March 2025. Kept for the
audit trail.

## Passwords

- A password must be at least 10 characters long.
- Every employee must change their password every 90 days.
- None of your last 5 passwords may be reused.

## Lockout

An account is locked after 5 failed sign-in attempts in a row, for 15 minutes.
The service desk can unlock it sooner after checking the employee's identity.
